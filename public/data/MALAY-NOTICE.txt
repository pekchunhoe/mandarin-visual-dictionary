KANJIAN Bahasa Melayu (Malaysia) additions

Original Mandarin, traditional spelling, pinyin and English definitions:
CC-CEDICT, https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cedict
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International:

The original dictionary corpus has not been changed by the Malay integration.

Malay glosses include preserved learning material, KANJIAN assistant-authored
exact-sense explanations and qualified Malaysian Malay terminology aligned with
Wordnet Bahasa (zsm), Chinese Open Wordnet and Princeton WordNet 3.0.
They require independent human linguistic review. No DBP/PRPM definitions were
scraped or reproduced. Model benchmark outputs were not mass-published.
No paid translation API was used. Dictionary additions are CC BY-SA 4.0;
individual records retain the additional source licenses, version and status.

filtered regional/grammatical alternatives and aligned them to unchanged
CC-CEDICT sense IDs using the separately licensed WordNet resources above.

illustrative example sentences or third-party dictionary extracts are reused.
